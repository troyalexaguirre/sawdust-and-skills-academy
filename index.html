Good habits start here

Safety is the first thing we build.

Confidence comes from knowing what to do, and when to ask for help. Every class starts with a safety check and a demonstration of the tools in use. Students who miss it will not be able to join the class, so please arrive on time.

For younger children, materials are pre-cut and power tools are instructor-only. Older students progress to supervised tool use only when ready, with instructor approval and a secure, clamped workpiece.
CPR certified, first aid on hand. Our instructors are CPR certified, and basic first aid supplies are kept on site and readily available.
  1. Protect your eyes.Wear properly fitting safety glasses whenever tools are in use.
  2. Listen first. Ask before using.Use a tool only after the instructor explains it and gives permission.
  3. Keep hands in the safe zone.Clamp wood securely; keep fingers clear of cutting paths and pinch points.
  4. Give tools and each other space.Walk, carry tools safely, and stay focused. No horseplay in the shop.
  5. Stop, tidy, and tell.Put tools away, keep the bench clear, and tell an instructor if anything feels unsafe.

Good questions

A few things parents ask.

New to woodworking? That’s exactly where many great makers start.

Does my child need any experience?

No experience is needed. The four age groups provide a starting point, and activities can be adapted to confidence, ability, and previous tool experience.

Will younger children use power tools?

No. Younger children’s materials are pre-cut, and power tools are instructor-only. Explorers focus on sanding, glue, clamps, decorating, and supported hand-tool use. Older groups progress only with instruction, readiness checks, and supervision.

Will they finish their project in class?

Classes aim for a finished or nearly finished take-home project. Pre-cut or partially prepared materials keep the focus on learning. Larger projects, such as a stool or bench, may need prepared assemblies or more than one session. Glue or finishes may still need drying time at home.

What should my child wear?

Closed-toe shoes and comfortable clothes that can get dusty. Tie back long hair, secure loose clothing, and remove dangling jewelry. Ask about protective equipment and any access needs before attending.

Is the class right for my child’s needs?

Kids of all abilities are welcome at Timber & Young. Tell us what your child needs, whether that’s learning, sensory, physical, or anything else, and we’ll work it out together. Our instructors share a belief that every child can build something they’re proud of.

Can parents stay?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to stay and watch, or to help their maker at the bench. Let us know about any access needs or other questions in your inquiry.

What if I need to cancel?

Email us at troy.alex.aguirre@gmail.com at least 48 hours before class to cancel. Because materials are purchased and prepared in advance, refunds are reviewed case by case and may be partial. Not every request qualifies, and Timber & Young makes the final decision. Cancellations due to weather or illness are not refunded unless discussed with our staff at least 48 hours before class. If we cancel a class, we'll offer a full refund or a credit toward another class.

What if we arrive late?

Classes start on time with a required safety briefing. Students who miss the safety portion will not be able to join the rest of the class, and no refund will be given. No exceptions, because safety comes first. Please plan to arrive a few minutes early.

What if I’m late for pickup?

Please pick up on time so our instructors can prepare for the next class. After 15 minutes, a $10 late fee may apply. If you’re running late, call or text (757) 968-3972.
